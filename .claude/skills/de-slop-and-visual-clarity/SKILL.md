# De-Slop & Visual Clarity Engineering Skill

Use this skill on **every** UI screen, NPC dialogue tree, quest prompt, tooltip, and 3D world build so the experience feels handcrafted by a senior game studio rather than generated by an LLM.

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Text & Dialogue De-Slop Rules
1. **NEVER Use Em-Dashes (`—`) or Double-Hyphen Stand-Ins (` -- `) in Game UI, Notifications, or Dialogue**:
   - Em-dashes are the #1 telltale fingerprint of raw LLM text in games.
   - Replace `—` with a clean period (`.`), a colon (`:`), a middle dot (`·`) for HUD stat pills, or split into two short lines.
2. **Ban AI Connector & Cliché Vocabulary**:
   - Never write: *"Moreover"*, *"Furthermore"*, *"Additionally"*, *"Consequently"*, *"Delve"*, *"Tapestry"*, *"Testament"*, *"Beacon"*, *"Symbiotic"*, *"Embark"*, *"Realm"*, *"Unleash"*, *"Elevate"*, *"It's not just X, it's Y"*, *"In conclusion"*, or *"Buckle up"*.
   - Write concrete, diegetic in-world language: `"Generator 2 offline. Flip the basement breaker."` instead of `"Furthermore, the generator — a vital beacon of power — requires your immediate attention!"`
3. **Strict Character Budgets per UI Element**:
   - **Button Label**: `1 to 3 words` (`<= 18 chars`, e.g., `"EQUIP"`, `"BUY · 250"`, `"REPAIR [E]"`).
   - **HUD Status Pill**: `<= 28 chars` (`"FLOOR 2 · 03:45"`).
   - **Tooltip / Objective Line**: `<= 75 chars` (one crisp action line, never a paragraph).

## 2. Visual Clarity Over Pure Text & Information Dumps
1. **Icon + Number + Color > Sentences**:
   - Instead of a text label reading `"Current Stamina: 85 / 100 (Regenerating)"`, use a lightning icon (`search_cloud_icons`), a fill bar (`Frame` with `UIGradient`), and `"85"`.
2. **Progressive Disclosure & Generous Whitespace**:
   - Never show more than **4 data points** inside a single card or HUD corner.
   - Keep total HUD screen coverage **under 18%** of the viewport so the 3D world stays center stage.
3. **Avoid Generic AI Color Palettes**:
   - Do not default to oversaturated `#a855f7` / `#00ffff` neon purple-on-black on every frame. Use intentional, high-contrast palettes (`generate_ui_theme_pack`) with consistent `UICorner` radii (`6px`, `10px`, or `12px`) and `1px–2px` `UIStroke` borders.

## 3. 3D World De-Slop: Prioritize 3D Meshes Over Raw Blocky `Part`s
1. **Meshes Must Be Prioritized**:
   - Never build furniture, trees, crates, lanterns, weapons, vehicles, or architectural props out of stacks of untextured rectangular `Part` blocks when the repo provides **520 commercial-safe 3D meshes** (`spawn_kit_mesh`, `search_assets(assetType="model")`, `import_rbxmx`)!
   - Use `Part`s for structural floors/walls/colliders, and dress every focal point with real 3D meshes (`MeshPart` / `.rbxmx` models) + surface materials + localized `PointLight`/`SpotLight`.
